# Quran Academee

> Quran Academee is a premier international online Quran academy dedicated to providing personalized live 1-on-1 Quran recitation, Tajweed, Hifz (memorization), Arabic language, and foundational Islamic studies for kids, teenagers, and adults worldwide under authentic scholarly supervision.

## 1. Overview & Mission
- **Official Domain:** https://www.quranacademee.com
- **Academic Supervision:** Directed under the supervision of Sheikh Jawwad Musa, graduate of the Islamic University of Madinah.
- **Global Coverage:** Providing 24/7 flexible scheduling tailored to students across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Europe, and the Middle East.
- **Teaching Format:** Personalized 1-on-1 live interactive virtual sessions utilizing digital Mushaf tools, screen sharing, and continuous student feedback.
- **Educators:** 100% verified certified male and female Quran scholars and Arabic educators. Female tutors are available upon request for female students and children.

## 2. Core Courses & Curricula
1. **Alqaida Almadania Basics / Noorani Qaida:** Foundational phonetics, Arabic alphabet recognition, Makharij (points of articulation), and basic reading rules for absolute beginners and young children.
2. **Quran Recitation & Reading:** Word-by-word Mushaf reading fluency, pronunciation perfection, breath management, and Waqf (stopping rules).
3. **Tajweed al Quran:** In-depth rules of classical Tarteel, Noon Sakinah & Tanween, Meem Sakinah, Mudood (prolongations), Qalqalah, and heavy/light letter rules.
4. **Quran Memorization (Hifz):** Structured memorization of the Holy Quran with personalized daily Sabaq (new lesson), Sabaqi (recent lessons revision), and Manzil (cumulative retention).
5. **Islamic Essentials & Duas:** Foundational Aqeedah (creed), practical Wudu and Salah step-by-step guidance, authentic daily Masnoon Duas, and Islamic manners (Akhlaq).
6. **Hifz Revision Partner:** Dedicated mentor program for Huffaz to cement memorization, resolve Mutashabihat (similar verses), and sustain lifelong retention.
7. **Arabic Language Course:** Classical Quranic and conversational Arabic, grammar (Nahw & Sarf), root-word vocabulary, and sentence construction to understand divine revelation directly.

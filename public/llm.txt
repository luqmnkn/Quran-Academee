# Quran Academee - Full Developer & AI Context Guide
> Quran Academee is a premier online educational platform dedicated to providing personalized, high-quality Quranic studies, Tajweed mastery, Arabic language instruction, and foundational Islamic learning for students worldwide.

## 1. Platform Overview & Mission
- **Domain:** [https://quranacademee.com](https://quranacademee.com)
- **Primary Mission:** To make authentic Quranic education accessible, engaging, and flexible for children, teenagers, and adults regardless of geographical location.
- **Teaching Format:** 1-on-1 live interactive online classes utilizing digital learning tools, screen sharing, and qualified male/female tutors.

## 2. Target Audience & Student Demographics
- **Children & Beginners:** Tailored interactive foundational courses (Qaida, basic reading, and short Surah memorization) with gentle, encouraging pacing.
- **Adult Learners:** Flexible schedules designed for working professionals or busy parents looking to learn reading from scratch, improve recitation, or understand Tajweed.
- **Advanced Students:** Focused programs for Hifz (memorization) and basic Tafseer (comprehension).

## 3. Core Courses & Curriculum Breakdown
- **Quran Reading with Tajweed:** Learning Arabic letters, proper pronunciation (Makharij), articulation points, and the precise rules of recitation.
- **Hifz-ul-Quran (Memorization):** Structured, step-by-step memorization of the Holy Quran with regular revision schedules and retention checks.
- **Arabic Language Foundation:** Basic vocabulary, grammar, and sentence structure designed to help students better understand the text of the Quran.
- **Islamic Studies:** Essential daily duas, Islamic etiquettes, and foundational knowledge suitable for growing minds.

## 4. Key Platform Features & Benefits
- **Flexible Timing:** Available 24/7 across various international time zones to fit family routines.
- **Customized Learning Plans:** Lesson pacing tailored to each student's current proficiency level and goals.
- **Trial Classes:** Offers trial sessions to help new students experience the teaching environment before committing.
- **Qualified Tutors:** Rigorously vetted instructors proficient in English, Urdu, and Arabic instruction.
